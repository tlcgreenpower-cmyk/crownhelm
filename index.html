A whole land to rule

Roam a wide open country of hills, rivers, forests and coasts. Build your realm into a beautiful city — then deal with your neighbouring kingdoms — up to seven of them — however you choose: trade gifts and keep the peace, or march your regiments to war and take their lands. Befriend them, or conquer every keep and rule the realm.

Controls
  • Left-drag select · Right-click move / attack / gather
  • WASD / arrows / edges pan · wheel zoom · hold wheel & drag look around · Shift+wheel-drag pan · Q / E rotate
  • C standard view · \ idle soldiers · M man size · F attack-move · R patrol · Z stance · B town bell · X stop · Ctrl+1–5 group · 1–5 recall
  • Double-click a unit = select all of its kind on screen
How to play
  • Keep → train Workers → gather Wood, Metal & Gold
  • Build your city: Houses, Farms, Mills, Markets, Churches, Castles
  • Realms (top bar) — gift gold to make friends, or declare war
  • Advance the Age to unlock Knights, Cannon & your champion
