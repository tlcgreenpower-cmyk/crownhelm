TIDEFALL

A whole land to rule. Roam a wide open country of hills, rivers, forests and coasts. Build your realm into a beautiful city — then deal with your three neighbouring kingdoms however you choose: trade gifts and keep the peace, or march your regiments to war and take their lands. Befriend them, or conquer every keep and rule the realm.

Controls

  • Left-drag select · Right-click move / attack / gather
  • Arrows / WASD / edges pan · Q / E rotate · wheel zoom
  • A attack-move · S stop · Ctrl+1–5 group · 1–5 recall
  • Double-click a unit = select all of its kind on screen

How to play

  • Keep → train Workers → gather Wood, Metal & Gold
  • Build your city: Houses, Farms, Mills, Markets, Churches, Castles
  • 🕊 Realms (top bar) — gift gold to make friends, or declare war
  • Advance the Age to unlock Knights, Cannon & your champion
