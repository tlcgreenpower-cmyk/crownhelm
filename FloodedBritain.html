TIDEFALL

The sea is taking Britain. Chop the drowning forests, salvage metal from the ruins, farm the high ground and raise an army before the water — and your rival across the estuary — wipe you out. Each tide rises higher than the last: the lowlands drown, and the war moves onto the water itself. Raze the enemy Keep before the flood claims you both.

Controls

  • Left-drag select · Right-click move / attack / gather
  • Arrow keys or screen edges — scroll the map (or click the minimap)
  • A attack-move · S stop · Ctrl+1–5 set group · 1–5 recall
  • Double-click a unit — select all of that type on screen

How to win

  • Keep → train Workers → chop Wood, mine Metal
  • Build Houses (pop), Farms (food), a Barracks for the army
  • Advance the Age at your Keep to unlock Knights, Catapults & Docks
  • When the lowlands flood, build a Dock and fight by boat
